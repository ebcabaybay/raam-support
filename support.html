iPhone: Raam doesn't appear after I start sharing

Tap Raam, then tap Start Broadcast in the screen-sharing sheet (choose Raam there if another app is selected). Raam then floats in a Picture in Picture window. If nothing appears, check that Picture in Picture is allowed in Settings › General › Picture in Picture. If it still won't appear, stop sharing, start again from Raam, or restart your iPhone to clear a stuck Picture in Picture window.

Translation is not appearing

Check that screen sharing is still on (the red indicator at the top of the screen) and that the text is clearly visible, not covered by Raam's window. Make sure the language you're reading is downloaded in Raam's list, or choose Auto-Detect to read every downloaded language at once. The first translation in a new language can take a moment while iOS gets ready.

A language says it needs downloading

Raam uses Apple's on-device translation. Tap the language in Raam's list and accept iOS's download. Downloaded languages are managed in Settings › Apps › Translate.

Android: Raam doesn't appear

Raam reads other apps through Android's Accessibility service. Open Raam, tap to start, and when Android's Accessibility settings open, find Raam under Downloaded apps and turn it on. If Raam still doesn't float over your apps, turn it off and on again in Settings › Accessibility › Raam, then start him again from the app.

Android: is the Accessibility permission safe?

Raam only uses it to read the text other apps show while he's out, and to draw his translations over them. Nothing he reads leaves your phone. Turn him off any time in Settings › Accessibility.

How do I stop sharing?

On iPhone, tap the red recording indicator at the top of the screen, or Raam's red screen-sharing control. On Android, double-tap Raam, or tap Come Home in the app, to bring him back.

Free minutes and Raam Unlimited

Raam reads for free for 10 minutes a day. Raam Unlimited removes the limit. On iPhone, subscriptions are handled by Apple: manage or cancel them in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, and restore purchases from Raam's settings. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

On Android, subscriptions are handled by Google Play: manage or cancel them in the Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. For refunds, see Google Play refunds.

How do I join the beta?

See Beta testing: TestFlight on iPhone, and a Google Group plus Google Play on Android.

Does Raam upload my screen?

No. Screen frames, the text Raam recognises and its translations are all processed on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else. See the privacy policy for details.
