Raam

Your screen,
in your language.

Raam swaps the words on your screen for your language, right where they were written, live, in any app. On iPhone and Android.

Recognition and translation happen entirely on your phone. No account, no server, and it works offline.

Beta testing

Raam is in beta on both platforms. Join, use him on the apps you actually read, and tell us what he gets wrong at raam.support@icloud.com.

iPhone, via TestFlight

  1. Open the TestFlight invite on your iPhone.
  2. Install Apple's TestFlight app if asked.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install.

Purchases in TestFlight are free, so you can try Raam Unlimited too.

Join the iPhone beta

Android, via Google Play

  1. Join the raam-testers Google Group with the Google account you use on your phone.
  2. Open the testing page and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Raam from Google Play. It can take a few minutes to show up.

Please stay in the beta for at least 14 days: Google needs that before Raam can go public. Purchases are real Google Play purchases.
