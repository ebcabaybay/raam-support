Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Raam, on iPhone and on Android, reads the text on your screen and translates it entirely on your device. The developer collects no data, and nothing on your screen is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

No collection

Raam has no account, analytics, advertising or server of its own. Screen contents, the text Raam recognises on them and their translations are not sent to the developer or to any third party, and are never sold or shared.

How Raam sees your screen

On iPhone, Raam sees your screen only after you tap him and confirm Start Broadcast in Apple's screen-sharing sheet. His broadcast extension passes the latest view to the app through one temporary frame in Raam's private App Group. It is replaced as the screen changes and deleted when sharing starts, ends or the app next opens.

On Android, Raam uses Android's Accessibility service, which you switch on yourself in Settings. While Raam is out, it lets him read the text other apps show and take screenshots so he can find words inside pictures. This is used only to translate what is on your screen, and the screenshots and text are processed in memory and never saved. The Accessibility service is not used for anything else.

Your screen can include sensitive information, such as messages, notifications, passwords, health or financial details. Raam treats all of it the same way: it is processed on your device only to translate it, and it never leaves the device.

Remembered translations

To show them faster, Raam remembers text he has translated together with its translation. On iPhone this is kept in Raam's own storage on your device, and iOS may clear it when space runs low. On Android it is kept only in memory while Raam is out, and is gone when he comes home. Either way, it never leaves your device.

Settings and usage

Your chosen languages, preferences and today's free minutes used are stored on your device only. Deleting Raam removes them.

Language downloads

On iPhone, translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework. Apple may download the assets for the languages you choose after asking for your permission. These downloads are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

On Android, text recognition and translation use Google's on-device ML Kit. The language models you choose are downloaded from Google. ML Kit may send Google basic information about its own use and performance, such as device type, app version and errors, but never your screen's contents, the text Raam finds or its translations. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and ML Kit's terms.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone, and by Google through Google Play on Android. Raam only learns from them whether a subscription is active; it never receives your payment details or your Apple or Google account information.

Your control

On iPhone, iOS shows a recording indicator the whole time you share your screen. You can stop from that indicator or from Raam's red screen-sharing control at any time.

On Android, double-tap Raam or tap Come Home in the app to bring him back, or triple-tap him to send him off. You can switch his Accessibility service off at any time in Settings › Accessibility.

Children

Raam is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
